# Research notes

These sources shaped the design. Read them yourself. We make no health claims.

## Why the layout must not change
- Sawyer and Palaniappan, 2025 (n=21): small user-interface changes lower usability and comfort for autistic users. https://kar.kent.ac.uk/110913
- W3C WAI user story: updates that move things cause stress. Tell users what changed and give them time to adjust. https://w3.org/WAI/people-use-web/user-stories/story-two/

## ADHD and reminders
- Interviews with 22 ADHD adults: reminders become invisible after a few days. Setup takes longer than the task. https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17258v1
- Some users forget to open the app. This is an open design question. We avoid push notifications by default, because many autistic users turn them off.

## Time signals and body doubling (later, opt-in)
- Users rely on simple signals: hourly beeps, shrinking visual timers.
- VR study with 12 ADHD adults: tasks finished faster with a human or AI body double. Small sample. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12153

## Labels and limits
- Several sources are small studies or preprints. Use them as hints only.
- No data yet shows that volunteer "nd-review" programmes work. M4 will give our first data.
