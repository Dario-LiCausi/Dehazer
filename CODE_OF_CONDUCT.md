# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a calm, safe place for everyone, including neurodivergent people.

## Do
- Be kind, clear and literal. Say what you mean.
- Assume good intent. Ask if something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect needs: some people want written replies, more time or fewer messages.

## Do not
- Insult, mock or diagnose anyone.
- Share private information about others.
- Harass, spam or pressure people.
- Make health claims or promise cures.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments and may block people who break these rules. Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer through GitHub.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
